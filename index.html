The problem with route planning

Planning deliveries is complex and slow. Spreadsheets and static tools can’t adapt to real‑time conditions, while constraints like capacity, driver shifts, and traffic change constantly.

  • Hours lost to manual planning
  • No real‑time re‑optimization
  • Higher mileage, fuel, and delays

Navvico’s solution

A conversational AI copilot that understands your constraints and optimizes in real time. Describe your deliveries and goals — Navvico plans, assigns, and keeps routes optimal as conditions change.

  • Chat‑first planning, no training needed
  • Live re‑optimization for traffic and status changes
  • Automatic vehicle assignment based on payload & shifts

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Navvico?

Navvico is a conversational, AI‑powered route optimization platform. It plans, assigns, and re‑optimizes deliveries in real time as conditions change.

How is it different from traditional planners?

It’s chat‑first, requires no training, and continuously re‑optimizes with live data like traffic, capacity, and driver status. Phone‑based driver tracking means no hardware costs.

How much does it cost?

Navvico is free for 3 months. After that, simple monthly plans that are cheaper than most competitors — with no hidden fees.

Do I need GPS devices or telematics?

No. Drivers use their smartphones for location and updates. No installations or per‑vehicle hardware charges.

Can it handle traffic for each delivery?

Yes. Navvico continuously optimizes each route and individual stops to avoid traffic and meet time windows.
